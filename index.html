Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about therapy and our practice at 60 6th Street Parkhurst, Randburg

How long is a typical session?

Individual sessions typically last 50-60 minutes. Initial consultations may sometimes run slightly longer to ensure a thorough understanding of your needs and to establish a safe therapeutic space at 60 6th Street Parkhurst or online.

Do you accept medical aid?

Yes, we are registered with the Board of Healthcare Funders (BHF) and can claim directly from most medical aids, subject to your specific plan and available benefits. We are also HPCSA registered – Health Professions Council of SA. BHF Practice Number: 0977969. Please bring your medical aid card.

How many sessions will I need?

The number of sessions varies depending on your individual goals. Some clients find benefit in short-term support (6-12 sessions), while others prefer longer-term therapeutic work. We review your progress together regularly to ensure meaningful and lasting change.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice will be charged at the full rate, as that time was reserved exclusively for you. Phone/WhatsApp: 067 389 0946 – contact us as soon as possible if you need to reschedule. Hours: In-person Saturdays 9am-2pm, Online Weekdays 4pm-6pm.
