Privacy Policy

POPIA Compliant – Your privacy matters deeply

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Hopeful Seasons Wellness is committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and HPCSA ethical guidelines.

2. Information We Collect

Contact details you provide via forms (name, email, phone, message), session notes (stored securely), and website analytics (anonymized).

3. How We Use Your Information

To respond to enquiries, schedule sessions, provide therapy, process medical aid claims (BHF), and improve our services. We never sell your data.

4. Confidentiality

All therapy content is confidential except where legally required (risk of harm, child protection, court order). We discuss limits of confidentiality in your first session.

5. Data Security & Retention

Records are stored encrypted, access-controlled, and retained per HPCSA requirements (minimum 6 years after last contact, or longer for minors). Supabase storage is secured.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and ethical obligations to maintain clinical records.

7. Contact

For privacy queries: info@hopefulseasons.co.za or Contact Page.
