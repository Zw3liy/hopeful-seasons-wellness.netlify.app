Your Journey, Your Pace

Individual therapy is a collaborative process where we explore thoughts, emotions, patterns, and experiences that shape your life. Whether you're facing overwhelming anxiety, persistent low mood, trauma, grief, or simply feeling stuck, therapy offers a space to be heard without judgment.

We Support With:

  • Anxiety, panic, and chronic worry
  • Depression, hopelessness, low motivation
  • Trauma, PTSD, and childhood wounds
  • Grief, loss, and bereavement
  • Low self-esteem and self-criticism
  • Life transitions, burnout, adjustment difficulties

Our Approach

We integrate client-centred, psychodynamic, and CBT frameworks. This means we honour your lived experience while gently exploring underlying patterns and equipping you with practical tools for daily life.
