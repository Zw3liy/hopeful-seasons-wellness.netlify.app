Family is a Living System

When one member struggles, the whole family feels it. Family therapy views challenges not as individual faults, but as patterns within the system that can be understood and shifted together.

We Help Families With:

  • Parent-child conflict and defiance
  • Adolescent mood, anxiety, or behavioural issues
  • Blended family and co-parenting challenges
  • Sibling rivalry and family conflict
  • Grief, divorce, relocation, or illness adjustments
  • Communication and boundary setting

Our Systems-Based Approach

Drawing from family systems theory, we explore roles, rules, and communication patterns. Sessions are collaborative and strength-based – we build on what's already working while gently challenging unhelpful cycles.
