Clarity Through Assessment

Psychological assessments provide a deeper understanding of functioning, diagnosis, capacity, or psycho-legal questions. All assessments are conducted in line with HPCSA ethical guidelines and best practice.

Types of Assessments:

  • Clinical psychological assessments for diagnostic clarification
  • Cognitive and emotional functioning evaluations
  • Psycho-legal assessments (with appropriate referral)
  • Personality and psychopathology assessments
  • Functional and capacity assessments
  • Risk assessment and mental health reports

What to Expect

Assessments typically involve 1-3 sessions plus scoring and report writing. You will receive clear explanation of the purpose, limits of confidentiality, and how results will be used. Reports are detailed, objective, and written in accessible language.

Referrals: We accept referrals from GPs, psychiatrists, attorneys, employers, and self-referrals. Please contact us to discuss suitability and fee structure for assessments, as they vary by complexity.
